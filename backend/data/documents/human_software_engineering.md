SOURCE: Stack Overflow
TITLE: 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey
AUTHOR/ORGANIZATION: Stack Overflow
DATE: July 29, 2025
URL: https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/
TOPIC: Developer trust in AI, human oversight, human judgment in software engineering

CONTENT:
Stack Overflow's 2025 Developer Survey collected responses from over
49,000 developers across 177 countries. It found that AI tool adoption
kept climbing: 84% of developers said they use or plan to use AI tools
in their development work, up from 76% the year before.

At the same time, trust in the accuracy of those tools fell sharply.
46% of developers said they actively distrust the accuracy of AI
tool output, compared to only 33% who said they trust it, and just 3%
reported "highly trusting" AI-generated results. Experienced developers
were the most cautious group of all, with the lowest rate of high trust
and the highest rate of high distrust, which the survey's authors read
as a sign of an ongoing need for human verification in roles where
someone is accountable for the final code.

The most commonly cited frustration, reported by 45% of respondents,
was that debugging AI-generated code that is "almost right, but not
quite" is often more time-consuming than writing the code from
scratch. When asked why they would still want to consult another human
even in a future where AI can handle most coding tasks, three quarters
of respondents said it was because they don't fully trust AI's
answers, and roughly six in ten cited security or ethical concerns, or
wanting to fully understand their own code.

RELEVANCE: Supports the case that human judgment, verification, and
accountability remain central to software engineering even as AI tool
usage grows (anti-replacement / human-oversight evidence).
