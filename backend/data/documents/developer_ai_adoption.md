TOPIC: Developer adoption of AI tools, adoption trends over time

CONTENT:
Stack Overflow has tracked developer attitudes toward AI tools with
consistent survey questions since 2023, which makes the year-over-year
trend a useful signal of how adoption and sentiment are evolving
together (or, increasingly, diverging).

In 2023, roughly 70% of developers reported using or planning to use AI
tools, with trust in their accuracy hovering around 40%. By 2025,
usage had climbed to 84% of developers, but reported trust in AI
accuracy had fallen to 29%, down from around 40% the prior year. Positive
overall sentiment toward AI tools also declined, from about 70-72% in
2023-2024 to roughly 60% in 2025. The survey's authors describe this as
a counterintuitive pattern -- normally, familiarity with a technology
increases confidence in it, but here rising usage has coincided with
falling trust.

The leading explanation offered is direct exposure to AI's failure
mode: 66% of developers report spending more time fixing code that is
"almost right, but not quite" than they would have spent writing it
themselves, and this front-and-center frustration appears to be
outweighing the convenience of AI-assisted drafting for many
respondents, even as adoption keeps climbing.

RELEVANCE: Directly relevant to how developers are adopting AI tools
over time, and complicates a simple narrative in either direction --
usage keeps growing (pro-AI), but trust keeps falling as usage grows
(AI-limitations), making this a genuinely balanced data point.
